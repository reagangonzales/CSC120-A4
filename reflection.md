My initial impression of encapsulation is that it significantly improved the organization and clarity of my code. It helped me separate concerns within my program by allowing each class to manage its own data while exposing only the necessary methods for interaction. However, the most challenging aspect was keeping track of how data was passed between objects across different classes.
Continuing to collaborate with classmates and attending TA hours worked well in this assignment. I had an issue figuring out where to put my main method to test the classes, and utilizing slack helped me figure this out. I would suggest to keep using/checking Slack to figure out answers to your questions. I'd also suggest writing down sudo code as it helped me keep track of each different class and how they were interacting with one another. Also doing what the README suggested and working on one class at a time helps you make small steps towards finishing the entire program.
